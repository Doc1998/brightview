Our Story

A Wicklow Business, Built on Honest Work

BrightView started with a simple idea — that homeowners in Wicklow deserved a roof cleaning company they could actually trust. No hard selling, no hidden charges, just quality work done right the first time.

We've been working on roofs across County Wicklow for over a decade. From Wicklow Town to Gorey, Enniscorthy to New Ross, we've cleaned, treated, and painted hundreds of roofs — and built our reputation one job at a time. We also regularly cover surrounding counties including Wicklow, Carlow, and Waterford, so don't hesitate to get in touch wherever you're based in the south east.

We're specialists in soft-wash roof cleaning and professional roof painting, using treatments that go beyond surface cleaning to tackle moss and algae at the root. Every job includes a full assessment, clear pricing upfront, and a tidy finish before we leave.
