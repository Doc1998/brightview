Service 01 Most Requested

Roof Cleaning

Moss, lichen, and algae don't just look bad — they trap moisture against your tiles, accelerating deterioration and leading to costly damage if left untreated. BrightView's professional roof cleaning service tackles the problem properly.

We use a low-pressure soft-wash system combined with a specialist biocide treatment. Unlike high-pressure jet washing — which can crack tiles, loosen mortar, and drive water into the roof structure — our method is safe for all tile types including concrete, slate, and clay.

  • ✓
    All tile types — concrete, slate, clay, and more
  • ✓
    Long-lasting results — treatments that keep working after we leave
  • ✓

Service 03 Specialist Treatment

Moss & Algae Removal

Ireland's wet climate makes moss and algae growth almost inevitable on roof tiles — especially on north-facing or shaded slopes. Left unchecked, moss lifts tiles, blocks gutters, and retains moisture that slowly degrades the roof structure beneath.

Our dedicated moss removal service goes beyond a basic clean. We apply a professional-grade biocide that penetrates deep into the moss and lichen colonies, killing them at the root. Over the weeks that follow, the dead growth washes away naturally in the rain — leaving a clean, treated surface.

  • ✓
    Deep-penetrating biocide — kills moss, algae, and lichen at the source
  • ✓
    Safe for all tile types — no abrasive scrubbing or high-pressure damage
  • ✓
    Continued action after treatment — keeps working long after we've left
  • ✓
    Can be paired with roof painting — for a clean, protected, great-looking finish

Service 05 Preventative Care

Gutter Cleaning

Blocked gutters are one of the most common — and most avoidable — causes of damp, fascia damage, and water ingress in Irish homes. When gutters overflow, water runs down the exterior walls and can seep into the structure of the building, leading to expensive repairs down the line.

BrightView's gutter cleaning service fully clears, flushes, and inspects your guttering from end to end. We check for cracks, sagging, and poor joints while we're up there, so you know exactly what condition your gutters are in.

  • ✓
    Full debris clearance — leaves, moss, and blockages removed throughout
  • ✓
    Downpipe flushing — ensures water flows freely all the way through
  • ✓
    Condition check included — we flag any cracks, sagging, or joint issues
  • ✓
    Often combined with roof cleaning — save on a visit and get both done together
